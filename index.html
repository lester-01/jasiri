Questions

Is it free?

The core is free to install and use, including for work. The terms say what that allows.

Do I need an AI API key?

Yes, for a cloud AI. You bring a key from OpenRouter, OpenAI or another compatible service. That service may charge you. You can also run a small model on the phone, which needs no key but is experimental and limited.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8 or later, on a 64-bit ARM64 phone, or on an x86_64 Android device or emulator on a PC. No root needed. A recent phone is more comfortable for longer jobs, and local models need memory.

Is it safe to install from outside Google Play?

Jasiri is signed with one key that stays the same from version to version. The Check page shows the file’s SHA-256, the signing fingerprint and a VirusTotal report, so you can confirm that what you downloaded is what was published.

Why isn’t Jasiri on Google Play yet?

Jasiri is a full computer on your phone, and a few of the things it can do, such as controlling other apps on your screen or installing an app file, are not allowed in apps on Google Play. This site gives you the full version, with nothing left out. A Google Play version will come later, with those few abilities removed. If you want everything now, use this site’s version. If you would rather have Play handle updates, the Play version is the one to wait for.

Can the AI delete my files?

It can, like any program you let run. Jasiri asks before risky commands, and files removed with the usual delete command go to a recycle bin for 30 days. Some ways of deleting from a program skip the bin, and that is one reason it asks first. Keep copies of what matters.

Where does my data go?

Your messages and attachments go to the AI service you picked, under its terms. Your files stay on your phone. Jasiri also sends crash reports and basic usage numbers to Google Firebase, which the app asks you to agree to when you first open it. They hold no chat text, files or keys; a crash report can occasionally include a fragment of technical error text. If you would rather not agree, you can export your data and leave. The Privacy page has the details.
